What are your delivery hours?

We deliver from 12:00 PM to 2:00 AM every day. For late-night cravings, we've got you covered! Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm availability.

Do you deliver to my area?

We primarily deliver in Aziz Nagar, L Block, Block D, and surrounding areas in Karachi. Message us on WhatsApp with your location to confirm delivery availability.

How can I place an order?

Ordering is easy! Just message us on WhatsApp at 0370 6823451 with your order details. We'll confirm and deliver your food hot and fresh.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery and online bank transfers. Payment details will be shared when you place your order via WhatsApp.

Do you offer any combo deals or discounts?

Yes! We have several value deals including Family Feast, Duo Combo, and Party Mega deals. Check our Deals section or ask us on WhatsApp for current offers.

We are located at L Block, Block D Aziz Nagar, Karachi, Pakistan. You can visit us or order for delivery via WhatsApp.
